The architectureProduct flow · Runtime · Integrations

The public-release reference design: Next.js + React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and background worker/scheduler services. Each arrow is labeled and points from the sender or caller to its destination. Planned features and experimental automation stay identifiable.

From candidate context to a considered next move

Arrows carry inputs or results. Candidate context and job requirements both reach evaluation before prioritization.

A reference design, with explicit boundaries.

The diagrams show responsibilities rather than exact queue transport or internal class boundaries. The scheduler initiates background work; the worker executes it. SQL arrows originate from the application process making the query.

Testing belongs outside the runtime flow.

Pytest checks backend behavior. Playwright supports browser/UI regression tests and may also drive the experimental browser agent. A test runner is not a downstream service called by the database or LLM.

Constraints & recommendations

Your preferences have a say.

Location, work mode, minimum compensation, work authorization, sponsorship, relocation, and seniority shape the recommendation. A hard conflict can outweigh strong technical alignment.

Contact intelligence · Early / partial

Find a useful conversation.

For promising jobs, search supported public professional and company sources for people whose public technical work appears closely related to the role. Coverage varies, and relevance stays separate from employment or a verified hiring relationship.

Sample contact: Daniel · Staff Engineer

Relevant because of public work in search and ranking. Employment and hiring responsibility remain unverified unless cited evidence proves them.

“Hi Daniel — I came across the Search Ranking opening and noticed the overlap with my retrieval and ranking work. I’d love to learn more about what the team is building.”

Application preparation

Make your experience clear.

  • Tailor a résumé using supported experience.
  • Draft answers grounded in candidate evidence.
  • Validate claims and review before submission.
  • Track opportunities and application progress.
ATS automation · Experimental

Manual mode is the default; auto-submit is off. The experimental Ashby workflow includes submission-state safeguards and receipt/idempotency handling. More adapters are planned.

Market & posting intelligence

Notice what changed.

Use stored roles to understand demand and compensation where it is available. Track posting history without turning patterns into unsupported claims.

  • First seen, last checked, and current status.
  • Repost events, description edits, and salary changes.
  • Role distribution, recurring skills, and strong-match counts.

Posting-change tracking is planned. Career counterfactuals—exploring how verified new skills might expand your options—remain a future capability.

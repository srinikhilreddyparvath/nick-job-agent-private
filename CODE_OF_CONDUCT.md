# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good intent, respect privacy, accept feedback gracefully, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Do not publish another person's private candidate, employment, credential, or application data.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [tonickred@gmail.com](mailto:tonickred@gmail.com). The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
